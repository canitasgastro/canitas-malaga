Rooftop bar in Málaga

The rooftop

Sunsets facing the Alcazaba

Málaga is best enjoyed from its rooftops, and few face the Alcazaba so directly. Cañitas al Fresco sits on the rooftop of ME Málaga by Meliá, next to Plaza de la Merced: a bar, open-air tables and a view that runs from the fortress to the Cathedral and the port.

Signature cocktails and a grazing menu by Cañitas Maite Restaurante: the Joselito ham croquette, homemade guacamole, white prawn Russian salad, truffled bikini toastie or Galician beef burger.

  • Opening hours Every day, 11 am to midnight.
  • No booking open to the public, first come, first served.
  • Pool reserved for hotel guests.
  • Best time sunset.
  • Menu in English and Spanish.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us

01Do I need to book at Cañitas al Fresco?

No. The rooftop does not take reservations: entry is first come, first served. Sunset is the busiest time, so come up early.

02Can I use the pool?

The rooftop pool is reserved for ME Málaga hotel guests. The Cañitas al Fresco rooftop bar, however, is open to everyone.

03Can I eat on the rooftop?

Yes. There are starters, gourmet sandwiches and sweets: homemade guacamole, white prawn Russian salad, bluefin tuna tartare, Galician beef burger… See the menu.

04Where is it?

On the rooftop of ME Málaga by Meliá, Calle Victoria 6, next to Plaza de la Merced, in Málaga city centre.

05Is the menu in English?

Yes, the menu is available in English and Spanish, on the rooftop and online.
