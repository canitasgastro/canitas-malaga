What data we process

We process the data you give us when booking, contacting us or making a purchase: name, phone number, email, number of guests, date and time, any preferences or allergies you tell us about and, where applicable, billing details. Allergy and intolerance information is used only to serve you safely.

Recipients and processors

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. To provide our service we rely on providers acting as data processors under GDPR-compliant agreements:

  • CoverManager: online booking system.
  • Web hosting and email providers.
  • Adobe Fonts (Adobe Inc.): web font service.
  • Payment platform (once the gift voucher shop goes live).

We do not share your data with ME Málaga by Meliá or any other company.

International transfers

If any provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, it will do so with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as adequacy decisions or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to canitasmalaga@melia.com stating which right you wish to exercise.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Minors

The services on this website are not aimed at children under 14. If you are under 14, please do not give us any data without your parents’ or guardians’ consent.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse or unauthorised access.
